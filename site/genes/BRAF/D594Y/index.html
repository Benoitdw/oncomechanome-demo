Wild type

BRAF is a RAS-regulated serine/threonine kinase in the MAPK pathway. In the absence of active RAS, wild-type BRAF exists as an autoinhibited monomer bound to MEK and 14-3-3 proteins. Upon RAS-GTP binding, it is recruited to the membrane, releases autoinhibition, and dimerizes. Within the active dimer, the highly conserved DFG motif at the start of the activation segment—where Asp594 is located—chelates the catalytic Mg2+ ion necessary to coordinate ATP and phosphorylate MEK1/2.

BRAF D594Y

The D594Y substitution replaces the catalytic DFG aspartate with a bulky aromatic tyrosine, removing the carboxylate group required for Mg2+ coordination. This destroys the kinase's ability to transfer a phosphate to MEK. However, the loss of this interaction at the active site destabilizes the autoinhibited monomeric state and shifts the kinase domain toward a conformation that favors dimerization.

Arbiter's reasoning

Steps for the loss of catalytic activity, increased binding to RAS-GTP, increased binding to CRAF, and the consequent increase in scaffolding function were merged from all three candidates. These events characterize the core signaling mechanism of Class III BRAF mutants. Candidate 3's steps for 'Conformational equilibrium shift' and 'Loss of autoinhibition' were included, as they are supported by mechanistic studies on D594 mutations detailing the physical basis of dimerization priming. Candidate 3's 'Increased protein abundance' step was dropped because it describes a co-transfection stabilization effect rather than a physiologically relevant steady-state increase of the mutant protein compared to wild-type. Candidate 3's 'Interactome rewiring' step was also dropped, as it is fully captured by the specific binding steps.

This is a demo of OncoMechanome

OncoMechanome annotates cancer variants with the chain of molecular mechanisms that links the amino-acid change to the change in protein activity. Each chain is built step by step by a panel of LLM annotators, every step backed by a cited source, then consolidated by an arbiter.

This demo shows a sample of annotated variants. The full version, covering the complete set of gain-of-function variants, will be available soon.
