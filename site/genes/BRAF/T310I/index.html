Not covered by the vocabulary

The only retrieved functional study (PMID: 31158244) characterizes T310I by its cellular output, showing it produces pERK levels comparable to wild-type BRAF and exhibits paradoxical activation by dabrafenib. Although the authors classify T310I as a 'low activity' (kinase-impaired) BRAF mutant based on this profile, they provide no direct biochemical measurement showing decreased intrinsic catalytic activity relative to wild-type BRAF, nor do they demonstrate any altered molecular interaction. Without direct evidence of a mutant-specific physical event, the mechanism cannot be annotated.

This is a useful answer, not a failure: how often it happens measures the coverage of the vocabulary.

Arbiter's reasoning

Candidate 1 correctly recognized that the only available functional data for T310I—cellular pERK levels comparable to wild-type BRAF—cannot support a directional label for a decrease in intrinsic activity. Although the authors of the primary study (PMID: 31158244) classify T310I as a 'low activity' mutant, this is an inference based on its failure to hyperactivate ERK and its paradoxical activation by the inhibitor dabrafenib. However, as Candidate 2 acknowledges, paradoxical activation is also a known property of wild-type BRAF. Because the study provides no direct in vitro catalytic measurement showing decreased activity relative to wild-type BRAF, nor does it establish an altered interaction or structural change, Candidates 2 and 3's proposed steps were dropped. Candidate 1's decision to leave the mechanism uncovered was adopted.

This is a demo of OncoMechanome

OncoMechanome annotates cancer variants with the chain of molecular mechanisms that links the amino-acid change to the change in protein activity. Each chain is built step by step by a panel of LLM annotators, every step backed by a cited source, then consolidated by an arbiter.

This demo shows a sample of annotated variants. The full version, covering the complete set of gain-of-function variants, will be available soon.
