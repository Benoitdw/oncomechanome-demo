Wild type

CACNA1C encodes the pore-forming alpha1C subunit of the CaV1.2 L-type voltage-gated calcium channel. Depolarization opens the pore, and calcium influx is subsequently restricted by two intrinsic negative-feedback mechanisms: voltage-dependent inactivation (VDI, where a hinged-lid mechanism involving the I-II linker closes the activation gate) and calcium-dependent inactivation (CDI, mediated by calcium binding to channel-associated calmodulin). CaV1.2 signaling is further tuned by the anchoring protein AKAP150, which clusters channels to facilitate coupled gating, and by RGK GTPases such as Gem, which bind the channel complex to inhibit calcium current and surface expression.

CACNA1C G406R

The G406R substitution replaces a highly conserved, flexible glycine with a bulky, positively charged arginine at the cytoplasmic end of the domain I S6 helix, immediately upstream of the I-II linker. This rigidifies the IS6 segment and stabilizes its open-state conformation, mechanically impending the normal closure of the inactivation gate. The new arginine also generates an RxxS consensus sequence for CaMKII phosphorylation.

Arbiter's reasoning

Merged the structural, electrophysiological, and macromolecular phenotypes from candidates 1 and 2. Adopted candidate 2's detailed breakdown of the electrophysiological deficits, including the separate reporting of contradictory findings on CDI (decreased maximal CDI versus sped CDI kinetics). Candidate 2's AKAP150 coupling steps and candidate 1's Gem-interaction steps were included to fully capture the mutant's altered macromolecular regulation. Edges were reconstructed to show how the structural rigidification of IS6, hyperpolarizing shift of activation, and novel phosphorylation site converge on impaired VDI, mode-2 gating, and ultimately the toxic sustained calcium influx that underlies Timothy syndrome.

This is a demo of OncoMechanome

OncoMechanome annotates cancer variants with the chain of molecular mechanisms that links the amino-acid change to the change in protein activity. Each chain is built step by step by a panel of LLM annotators, every step backed by a cited source, then consolidated by an arbiter.

This demo shows a sample of annotated variants. The full version, covering the complete set of gain-of-function variants, will be available soon.
