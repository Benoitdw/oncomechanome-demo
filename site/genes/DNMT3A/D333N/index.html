Wild type

DNMT3A is a de novo DNA methyltransferase that deposits methylation on CpG dinucleotides. Its N-terminal PWWP domain recognizes dimethylated and trimethylated histone H3 lysine 36 (H3K36me2/3), which targets the enzyme to H3K36-methylated intergenic and gene-body chromatin while keeping it away from Polycomb-regulated CpG islands. The PWWP domain also mediates intramolecular autoinhibition by docking onto the ADD domain, occluding the enzyme's active site until histone binding relieves this restraint. Additionally, an N-terminal ubiquitin-dependent recruitment region can bind H2AK119ub, though the PWWP-H3K36 interaction normally outcompetes this targeting mechanism.

DNMT3A D333N

The D333N substitution replaces the negatively charged aspartate residue in the PWWP domain's aromatic cage with an uncharged asparagine. This residue normally coordinates both the positively charged methyl-lysine of H3K36 and a corresponding arginine from the ADD domain during autoinhibition.
