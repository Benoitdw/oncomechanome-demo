This is a useful answer, not a failure: how often it happens measures the coverage of the vocabulary.

Arbiter's reasoning

All three candidates returned an uncovered graph because no primary literature details the specific molecular behavior of EGFR M178I, and their conclusion was accepted. Although the variant was included in a high-throughput viability screen (Ng et al. 2018) and is listed as 'Likely Gain-of-function' in OncoKB, no retrieved evidence describes its physical effect on receptor conformation, dimerization, or kinase activation. The rules prohibit inferring molecular steps solely from a database classification or a cellular viability phenotype, requiring that the mechanism remain uncovered. The wild-type and substitution descriptions were synthesized from the candidates to frame this evidence gap.

This is a demo of OncoMechanome

OncoMechanome annotates cancer variants with the chain of molecular mechanisms that links the amino-acid change to the change in protein activity. Each chain is built step by step by a panel of LLM annotators, every step backed by a cited source, then consolidated by an arbiter.

This demo shows a sample of annotated variants. The full version, covering the complete set of gain-of-function variants, will be available soon.
