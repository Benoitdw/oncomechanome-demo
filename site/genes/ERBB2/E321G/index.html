Wild type

ERBB2 (HER2) is a receptor tyrosine kinase of the epidermal growth factor (EGF) receptor family. Although it lacks a known cognate ligand, its extracellular domain exists in a conformation that readily heterodimerizes with ligand-bound EGFR, HER3, or HER4. Dimerization brings the intracellular kinase domains together, enabling trans-autophosphorylation and subsequent downstream signaling pathways that promote cell proliferation. The extracellular subdomain II participates in receptor dimerization and is stabilized by 11 intramolecular disulfide bonds and internal salt bridges, including one formed between E321 and K369/R434.

ERBB2 E321G

The substitution of glutamate for glycine at position 321 removes the acidic side chain required for salt bridge formation with K369 and R434. This disrupts the stabilizing intramolecular contacts in extracellular subdomain II, loosening the structure of its disulfide-bonded loops and freeing cysteines that normally form intramolecular bonds.
