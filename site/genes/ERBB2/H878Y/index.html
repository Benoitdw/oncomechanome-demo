Wild type

HER2 (ERBB2) is a receptor tyrosine kinase that acts as a preferred heterodimerization partner for other ErbB family members. Upon dimerization, its intrinsic kinase is activated, leading to autophosphorylation of C-terminal tyrosines that recruit downstream signaling effectors. The kinase domain is normally regulated by the conformation of its activation loop, which features a histidine at position 878.

ERBB2 H878Y

The substitution replaces histidine 878 in the activation loop with a tyrosine, introducing a new phosphorylatable hydroxyl group adjacent to the existing wild-type tyrosine 877.

This is a demo of OncoMechanome

OncoMechanome annotates cancer variants with the chain of molecular mechanisms that links the amino-acid change to the change in protein activity. Each chain is built step by step by a panel of LLM annotators, every step backed by a cited source, then consolidated by an arbiter.
