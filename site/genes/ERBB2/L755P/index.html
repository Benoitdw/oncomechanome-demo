Wild type

ERBB2 (HER2) is a receptor tyrosine kinase of the ErbB family that lacks a known direct ligand but forms active heterodimers with ligand-bound EGFR, ERBB3, or ERBB4. In its inactive state, the kinase domain is autoinhibited with helix αC shifted outward. Residue Leu755 is located in the N-lobe on the β3 strand next to the αC helix, where its side chain packs against helix αC and its backbone amide hydrogen-bonds with Val790 on the β5 strand to stabilize the local β-sheet. Type II inhibitors like lapatinib specifically bind the inactive kinase conformation.

ERBB2 L755P

The substitution of proline for leucine at position 755 replaces the hydrophobic side chain and removes the backbone amide hydrogen. This breaks the β3–β5 backbone hydrogen bond to Val790, locally destabilizing the β-sheet, altering the packing against helix αC, and triggering structural rearrangements of the hinge region and the glycine-rich P-loop.
