Wild type

ERBB2 (HER2) is a receptor tyrosine kinase that does not bind a cognate ligand. Instead, it normally functions by heterodimerizing with ligand-bound members of the epidermal growth factor receptor (EGFR) family, such as ERBB3 or ERBB4. Dimerization activates its intracellular kinase domain, which trans-phosphorylates the receptor's C-terminal tail and stimulates downstream signaling cascades, including the MAPK/ERK and PI3K/AKT pathways, to regulate cell proliferation and survival. The extracellular domain contains a cysteine-rich subdomain II that mediates receptor dimerization and is ordinarily held in a conformation that restrains basal signaling.

ERBB2 V272G

The substitution replaces valine 272 with glycine in the extracellular cysteine-rich subdomain II, a region involved in receptor dimerization. This removes a hydrophobic side chain and introduces local backbone flexibility, leading to constitutive activation without a resolved structural mechanism.

Arbiter's reasoning

The candidates agree on the activating nature of V272G based on an ASCO abstract demonstrating increased downstream pathway signaling in reporter assays. Candidates 1 and 2 assigned 'Increase in intrinsic activity' with 'of: catalytic', which was retained over candidate 3's 'of: effector engagement' because ERBB2 is a receptor tyrosine kinase whose oncogenic gain-of-function primarily stems from its constitutive kinase activity. The step was merged and its note synthesized to reflect the receptor's enhanced ability to drive signaling. No quantities were extracted because the abstract does not report numerical data.

This is a demo of OncoMechanome

OncoMechanome annotates cancer variants with the chain of molecular mechanisms that links the amino-acid change to the change in protein activity. Each chain is built step by step by a panel of LLM annotators, every step backed by a cited source, then consolidated by an arbiter.

This demo shows a sample of annotated variants. The full version, covering the complete set of gain-of-function variants, will be available soon.
