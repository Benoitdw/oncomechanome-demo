Wild type

ERBB3 (HER3) is a kinase-impaired receptor tyrosine kinase that acts as a signaling scaffold. In the absence of its ligand neuregulin-1 (NRG1), its extracellular region adopts an autoinhibited, tethered conformation. Upon ligand binding, the receptor extends and heterodimerizes preferentially with the kinase-active ERBB2. ERBB2 then transphosphorylates the ERBB3 C-terminal tail, providing docking sites that recruit the p85 subunit of PI3K to activate the PI3K/AKT and ERK signaling pathways.

ERBB3 A232V

Valine replaces alanine 232 in the extracellular subdomain II, at the interface with subdomain I. This substitution alters interdomain packing, destabilizing the autoinhibited tethered state and predisposing the receptor to adopt an extended, dimerization-competent conformation without requiring a ligand.

This is a demo of OncoMechanome

OncoMechanome annotates cancer variants with the chain of molecular mechanisms that links the amino-acid change to the change in protein activity. Each chain is built step by step by a panel of LLM annotators, every step backed by a cited source, then consolidated by an arbiter.

This demo shows a sample of annotated variants. The full version, covering the complete set of gain-of-function variants, will be available soon.
