Wild type

FXYD2 (the gamma subunit of the Na,K-ATPase) is a small type I single-span membrane protein expressed in the distal nephron. Its transmembrane helix assembles with the Na,K-ATPase alpha-beta heterodimer, allowing FXYD2 to be routed to the basolateral plasma membrane where it tunes the pump's kinetic parameters by increasing its apparent ATP affinity (lowering K'(ATP)) and altering its apparent Na+ affinity. Wild-type FXYD2 also self-associates into homo-oligomers and confers a non-selective transepithelial cation conductance.

FXYD2 G41R

The G41R substitution replaces a highly conserved small transmembrane glycine with a bulky, positively charged arginine. This introduces a fixed positive charge into the membrane-spanning helix and disrupts the hydrophobic packing face required for association with the Na,K-ATPase alpha subunit.

Arbiter's reasoning

The final graph merges steps from all candidates. Steps for the loss of association with the Na,K-ATPase, misrouting to intracellular compartments, and the resulting loss of functional pump modulation were retained. The dominant-negative interference via co-oligomerization with wild-type FXYD2 was included. The observations of novel Mg2+-dependent inward-rectifying channel gating and decreased transepithelial transport were retained from candidates 1 and 2, as was candidate 1's step capturing decreased steady-state protein abundance due to degradation. Candidate 2's step reporting a loss of self-oligomerization for an isolated transmembrane peptide was dropped, because the full-length mutant protein is shown to retain self-oligomerization.

This is a demo of OncoMechanome

OncoMechanome annotates cancer variants with the chain of molecular mechanisms that links the amino-acid change to the change in protein activity. Each chain is built step by step by a panel of LLM annotators, every step backed by a cited source, then consolidated by an arbiter.

This demo shows a sample of annotated variants. The full version, covering the complete set of gain-of-function variants, will be available soon.
