Wild type

FXYD2 (the gamma subunit of the Na,K-ATPase) is a small type I single-span membrane protein expressed in the distal nephron. Its transmembrane helix assembles with the Na,K-ATPase alpha-beta heterodimer, allowing FXYD2 to be routed to the basolateral plasma membrane where it tunes the pump's kinetic parameters by increasing its apparent ATP affinity (lowering K'(ATP)) and altering its apparent Na+ affinity. Wild-type FXYD2 also self-associates into homo-oligomers and confers a non-selective transepithelial cation conductance.

FXYD2 G41R

The G41R substitution replaces a highly conserved small transmembrane glycine with a bulky, positively charged arginine. This introduces a fixed positive charge into the membrane-spanning helix and disrupts the hydrophobic packing face required for association with the Na,K-ATPase alpha subunit.
