Wild type

HCN4 assembles into tetrameric channels that carry the hyperpolarization-activated 'funny' current (If), which drives diastolic depolarization in sinoatrial node pacemaker cells. Channel opening is triggered by membrane hyperpolarization and is further facilitated by the direct binding of cAMP to a cytoplasmic cyclic nucleotide-binding domain. The cAMP signal is transmitted to the pore via an intervening C-linker.

HCN4 D553N

The D553N missense substitution replaces a conserved charged aspartate with an uncharged asparagine in the C-linker gating ring. This removes a negative charge and alters local electrostatic interactions, which has been proposed to either cause misfolding that prevents forward trafficking or to stabilize a closed conformation of the gate.

Arbiter's reasoning

I merged steps from the candidates to reflect the two conflicting mechanisms documented in the literature. I kept the 'Interference with the wild-type protein' step describing the dominant-negative trafficking defect initially reported. From the later study finding normal trafficking, I kept the 'Conformational equilibrium shift' and 'Decrease in intrinsic activity' steps to capture the intrinsic C-linker gating defect. I also retained the 'Loss of response to a cognate signal' step for the altered cAMP regulation. I dropped the 'Change of localization' and 'Folding destabilization' steps because their slot values poorly fit a folding-mediated ER retention, and the functional consequence is fully captured by the interference step. Quantities extracted from tables without supporting prose were removed.

This is a demo of OncoMechanome

OncoMechanome annotates cancer variants with the chain of molecular mechanisms that links the amino-acid change to the change in protein activity. Each chain is built step by step by a panel of LLM annotators, every step backed by a cited source, then consolidated by an arbiter.

This demo shows a sample of annotated variants. The full version, covering the complete set of gain-of-function variants, will be available soon.
