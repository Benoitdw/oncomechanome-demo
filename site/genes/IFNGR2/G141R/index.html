Wild type

IFNGR2 encodes IFN-γR2, the non-ligand-binding, signal-transducing β chain of the interferon-γ receptor. The wild-type extracellular domain is N-glycosylated and folds in the endoplasmic reticulum before passing glycan-dependent quality control. The mature, fully processed chain is transported to the plasma membrane, where it complexes with IFN-γ-bound IFN-γR1 to recruit JAK2, driving STAT1 phosphorylation and GAS-dependent transcription.

IFNGR2 G141R

The substitution replaces a small, conserved glycine with a bulky, positively charged arginine in the short linker connecting the two fibronectin type III domains of the extracellular region. This sterically and electrostatically incompatible side chain prevents the nascent receptor from reaching its native conformation.

This is a demo of OncoMechanome

OncoMechanome annotates cancer variants with the chain of molecular mechanisms that links the amino-acid change to the change in protein activity. Each chain is built step by step by a panel of LLM annotators, every step backed by a cited source, then consolidated by an arbiter.

This demo shows a sample of annotated variants. The full version, covering the complete set of gain-of-function variants, will be available soon.
