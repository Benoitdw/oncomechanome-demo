Wild type

IFNGR2 encodes IFN-γR2, the non-ligand-binding accessory chain of the interferon-γ (IFN-γ) receptor. It matures through the secretory pathway, acquiring N-linked glycans before reaching the plasma membrane. There, it assembles with the ligand-binding IFN-γR1 chain. Upon IFN-γ binding, the assembled receptor complex recruits and activates JAK kinases, leading to STAT1 phosphorylation and the transcription of IFN-γ-responsive genes that drive macrophage antimicrobial immunity.

IFNGR2 G227R

The G227R substitution introduces a bulky, positively charged arginine into the membrane-proximal D2 fibronectin type III domain of the extracellular region. This disrupts local folding, causing the newly synthesised chain to be trapped in the endoplasmic reticulum with immature, untrimmed high-mannose glycans. This severely reduces the amount of mature receptor that reaches the cell surface, leaving only a functionally impaired, abnormally glycosylated population at the plasma membrane.

Arbiter's reasoning

All three candidates correctly described the variant's mechanism as a hypomorph caused by misfolding and abnormal N-glycosylation, which leads to endoplasmic reticulum retention and a decreased pool of mature cell-surface receptor. Candidate 2's detailed structural placement in the D2 fibronectin type III domain was adopted for the folding step. The steps were consolidated into folding destabilization, decreased abundance via impaired maturation, and a decrease in effector engagement activity. The 53% residual activity reported in PMID:23963039 was selected as the quantity for the functional defect, as it was the most clearly sourced figure among the candidates.

This is a demo of OncoMechanome

OncoMechanome annotates cancer variants with the chain of molecular mechanisms that links the amino-acid change to the change in protein activity. Each chain is built step by step by a panel of LLM annotators, every step backed by a cited source, then consolidated by an arbiter.

This demo shows a sample of annotated variants. The full version, covering the complete set of gain-of-function variants, will be available soon.
