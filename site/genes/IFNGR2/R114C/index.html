Wild type

IFNGR2 encodes IFN-γR2, the signal-transducing chain of the heterodimeric IFN-γ receptor. The wild-type chain is synthesized in the secretory pathway, undergoes N-glycosylation and maturation, and is transported to the plasma membrane. There, it associates with the ligand-binding IFN-γR1 chain so that extracellular IFN-γ binding triggers JAK1/JAK2 activation, STAT1 tyrosine phosphorylation and nuclear translocation, and downstream responses such as HLA-DR upregulation and IL-12 production.

IFNGR2 R114C

The R114C substitution replaces an arginine residue that forms part of a conserved π-cation stacking motif in the extracellular D1 fibronectin type-III domain. The loss of this arginine side chain and introduction of an unpaired cysteine disrupts the stability of this structural core motif, leading to misfolding of the ectodomain.

This is a demo of OncoMechanome

OncoMechanome annotates cancer variants with the chain of molecular mechanisms that links the amino-acid change to the change in protein activity. Each chain is built step by step by a panel of LLM annotators, every step backed by a cited source, then consolidated by an arbiter.

This demo shows a sample of annotated variants. The full version, covering the complete set of gain-of-function variants, will be available soon.
