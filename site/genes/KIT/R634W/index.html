Wild type

Wild-type KIT is a receptor tyrosine kinase that requires its ligand, stem cell factor (SCF), for activation. Binding of SCF induces receptor dimerization and trans-autophosphorylation, initiating downstream signaling pathways such as STAT, PI3K/AKT, and MAPK to regulate cell survival and proliferation.

KIT R634W

The R634W substitution replaces a basic arginine with a bulky, aromatic tryptophan in the KIT kinase domain, which uncouples the receptor's kinase activity from its normal requirement for ligand binding.

This is a demo of OncoMechanome

OncoMechanome annotates cancer variants with the chain of molecular mechanisms that links the amino-acid change to the change in protein activity. Each chain is built step by step by a panel of LLM annotators, every step backed by a cited source, then consolidated by an arbiter.

This demo shows a sample of annotated variants. The full version, covering the complete set of gain-of-function variants, will be available soon.
