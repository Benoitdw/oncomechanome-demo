Wild type

NRAS is a small GTPase that operates as a molecular switch, cycling between an inactive GDP-bound state and an active GTP-bound state to relay extracellular signals to downstream pathways like the MAPK cascade. Activation occurs when guanine nucleotide exchange factors (GEFs) promote GDP release and GTP loading. In the active state, the switch I and switch II regions close around the nucleotide and a catalytic magnesium ion, enabling interactions with effectors such as RAF kinases. Signalling is terminated when GTPase-activating proteins (GAPs) accelerate the intrinsically slow hydrolysis of GTP back to GDP.

NRAS A59T

The substitution replaces alanine 59, which sits in the switch II region of the active site, with threonine. The bulkier side chain and its introduced hydroxyl group disrupt the local hydrogen-bonding network, displacing the catalytic water molecule. Furthermore, the threonine positions itself near the gamma-phosphate of bound GTP to act as an intramolecular phosphate acceptor.

Arbiter's reasoning

Candidate 1 was rejected for leaving the variant uncovered due to the lack of direct measurements on NRAS; however, the literature establishes that Ala59 is perfectly conserved across RAS isoforms and functional data for K-RAS and H-RAS A59T are generalisable. Candidates 2 and 3 correctly extrapolated these mechanisms. I merged their steps, grouping individual RAF isoforms into a single step for RAF proteins, and maintained the evidence from H-RAS and K-RAS studies. Conflicting reports of SOS1 affinity changes were accommodated as a decrease in binding affinity.

This is a demo of OncoMechanome

OncoMechanome annotates cancer variants with the chain of molecular mechanisms that links the amino-acid change to the change in protein activity. Each chain is built step by step by a panel of LLM annotators, every step backed by a cited source, then consolidated by an arbiter.

This demo shows a sample of annotated variants. The full version, covering the complete set of gain-of-function variants, will be available soon.
