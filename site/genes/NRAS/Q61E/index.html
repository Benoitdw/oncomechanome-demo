Wild type

NRAS is a plasma membrane-localized small GTPase that cycles between an inactive GDP-bound state and an active GTP-bound state. In the active state, it engages downstream effectors such as RAF, and signaling is terminated by intrinsic GTP hydrolysis, which is accelerated by orders of magnitude upon binding to a GTPase-activating protein (GAP). Glutamine 61 in the switch II region is a critical catalytic residue that positions a water molecule for nucleophilic attack on the GTP gamma-phosphate and interfaces with the GAP arginine finger.

NRAS Q61E

The Q61E substitution replaces the neutral glutamine amide with a negatively charged glutamate carboxylate in the switch II region, placing a full negative charge adjacent to the GTP gamma-phosphate and the catalytic water molecule.
