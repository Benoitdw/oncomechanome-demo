Wild type

PDGFRA encodes a receptor tyrosine kinase. Binding of its cognate PDGF ligand dimerizes the receptor, relieving the autoinhibitory restraint of the intracellular juxtamembrane segment and triggering trans-autophosphorylation of the kinase domains to activate downstream signaling pathways.

PDGFRA S584L

The S584L substitution replaces a polar serine with a bulky, hydrophobic leucine in the intracellular juxtamembrane region. This alteration leads to partial auto-activation of the receptor.

Arbiter's reasoning

Candidates 1, 2, and 3 were merged. S584L was reported among the mutants that showed weak to medium phosphorylation without PDGF stimulation. I included 'Loss of regulatory dependence' (independent of ligand) as it captures the ligand-independent autophosphorylation, and 'Increase in intrinsic activity' (catalytic) to represent the elevated basal kinase activity. Candidate 2's edge was reversed, as the loss of ligand dependence causes the basal kinase activity to be elevated, rather than the reverse. Candidate 3's omission of 'Loss of autoinhibition' was maintained, as no direct evidence confirms the structural mechanism of relief of autoinhibition for S584L specifically.

This is a demo of OncoMechanome

OncoMechanome annotates cancer variants with the chain of molecular mechanisms that links the amino-acid change to the change in protein activity. Each chain is built step by step by a panel of LLM annotators, every step backed by a cited source, then consolidated by an arbiter.

This demo shows a sample of annotated variants. The full version, covering the complete set of gain-of-function variants, will be available soon.
