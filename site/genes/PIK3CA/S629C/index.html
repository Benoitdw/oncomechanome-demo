Not covered by the vocabulary

The available functional evidence for S629C is limited to a high-throughput screen (PMID: 29533785) that reports increased factor-independent proliferation in Ba/F3 and MCF10A cell models. No primary source has measured the direct molecular mechanism—such as altered p85 binding, intrinsic lipid kinase activity, or downstream p-AKT signaling—underlying this cellular phenotype.

This is a useful answer, not a failure: how often it happens measures the coverage of the vocabulary.

Arbiter's reasoning

Candidate_3's steps were dropped because they incorrectly annotate extracellular growth factors (IL-3 and EGF) as direct targets of PIK3CA, an intracellular kinase. While the cited high-throughput screen (PMID: 29533785) demonstrates that S629C promotes factor-independent cell proliferation, this is a downstream cellular phenotype rather than a molecular event on the mutant protein itself. As candidate_1 and candidate_2 observed, there is no primary literature reporting a specific biochemical mechanism—such as a change in p85 regulatory binding or an in vitro lipid kinase activity measurement—for the S629C substitution. Without evidence characterizing the molecular mechanism, the variant is not covered.

Rejected in whole: candidate_3

This is a demo of OncoMechanome

OncoMechanome annotates cancer variants with the chain of molecular mechanisms that links the amino-acid change to the change in protein activity. Each chain is built step by step by a panel of LLM annotators, every step backed by a cited source, then consolidated by an arbiter.

This demo shows a sample of annotated variants. The full version, covering the complete set of gain-of-function variants, will be available soon.
