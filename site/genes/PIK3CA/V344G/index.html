Wild type

PIK3CA encodes p110α, the catalytic subunit of phosphoinositide 3-kinase (PI3K) alpha. It associates with a p85 regulatory subunit (e.g. PIK3R1) to form a holoenzyme heterodimer. In the basal state, p110α lipid kinase activity is autoinhibited by the p85 nSH2 and iSH2 domains; specifically, the iSH2 domain forms an inhibitory contact with the p110α C2 domain. PI3Kα is activated when the p85 SH2 domains bind to phosphorylated YxxM motifs on activated receptor tyrosine kinases (such as ERBB3) or adaptor proteins (like IRS1 and GAB1), which relieves autoinhibition, recruits the complex to the plasma membrane, and allows p110α to phosphorylate PI(4,5)P2 to PIP3.

PIK3CA V344G

The V344G substitution replaces a buried valine with a glycine within the p110α C2 domain. This removes a bulky hydrophobic side chain, destabilizing a hydrophobic network in the C2 domain at its interface with the p85α iSH2 domain.

This is a demo of OncoMechanome

OncoMechanome annotates cancer variants with the chain of molecular mechanisms that links the amino-acid change to the change in protein activity. Each chain is built step by step by a panel of LLM annotators, every step backed by a cited source, then consolidated by an arbiter.

This demo shows a sample of annotated variants. The full version, covering the complete set of gain-of-function variants, will be available soon.
