Wild type

PIK3CA encodes p110α, the catalytic subunit of phosphoinositide 3-kinase (PI3K) alpha. It associates with a p85 regulatory subunit (e.g. PIK3R1) to form a holoenzyme heterodimer. In the basal state, p110α lipid kinase activity is autoinhibited by the p85 nSH2 and iSH2 domains; specifically, the iSH2 domain forms an inhibitory contact with the p110α C2 domain. PI3Kα is activated when the p85 SH2 domains bind to phosphorylated YxxM motifs on activated receptor tyrosine kinases (such as ERBB3) or adaptor proteins (like IRS1 and GAB1), which relieves autoinhibition, recruits the complex to the plasma membrane, and allows p110α to phosphorylate PI(4,5)P2 to PIP3.

PIK3CA V344G

The V344G substitution replaces a buried valine with a glycine within the p110α C2 domain. This removes a bulky hydrophobic side chain, destabilizing a hydrophobic network in the C2 domain at its interface with the p85α iSH2 domain.

Arbiter's reasoning

Merged the mechanisms from all three candidates to construct a unified graph. The core structural mechanism was taken from all candidates, which trace the destabilization of the C2 hydrophobic network to a weakened C2-iSH2 interaction and a loss of autoinhibition (PMID:34779417). The subsequent effect on membrane binding was included as proposed by candidates 2 and 3. The downstream effect on protein interactions (ERBB3, GAB1, IRS1) reported by PMID:34591642 was integrated from candidates 1 and 3. The edge from loss of autoinhibition to these increased interactions was maintained from candidate 3, as it accurately reflects how the relief of p85-imposed restraints makes the p85 SH2 domains more available for binding YxxM motifs. The interactome rewiring step was kept but mapped to 'toward: shifted target set'.

This is a demo of OncoMechanome

OncoMechanome annotates cancer variants with the chain of molecular mechanisms that links the amino-acid change to the change in protein activity. Each chain is built step by step by a panel of LLM annotators, every step backed by a cited source, then consolidated by an arbiter.

This demo shows a sample of annotated variants. The full version, covering the complete set of gain-of-function variants, will be available soon.
