Wild type

PIK3CB encodes p110β, the catalytic subunit of class IA phosphoinositide 3-kinase beta (PI3Kβ). It forms an obligate heterodimer with a p85 regulatory subunit, which holds the enzyme in an autoinhibited state through intramolecular contacts. Upon relief of this inhibition by upstream signals such as receptor tyrosine kinases, G-protein coupled receptors, or small GTPases, p110β binds the cell membrane and phosphorylates phosphatidylinositol 4,5-bisphosphate (PIP2) to generate the second messenger phosphatidylinositol 3,4,5-trisphosphate (PIP3). PIP3 then recruits downstream effectors, including AKT and PDK1, to promote cell survival, proliferation, and growth.

PIK3CB D1067Y

The D1067Y substitution replaces a negatively charged aspartate with a bulky, uncharged aromatic tyrosine near the extreme C-terminus of the p110β kinase domain. While the specific disrupted structural contact is not resolved in the literature, the mutation alters a critical regulatory segment of the kinase domain and acts as a strongly activating lesion.

Arbiter's reasoning

All three candidates correctly identified that the D1067Y substitution leads to hyperactivation of the p110β lipid kinase. I kept the 'Increase in intrinsic activity' step supported by all candidates, using the valid quotes from PMID:26759240. Candidate 1's 'Loss of regulatory dependence' step was dropped because the cited 'constitutive activation' is not explicitly distinguished in the retrieved sources from the general increase in intrinsic catalytic activity, and the structural mechanism of activation remains unresolved. Evidence quotes from Candidate 2 and Candidate 3 that relied on unprovided PMCIDs or sources absent from the retrieved texts were excluded from the final annotation.

This is a demo of OncoMechanome

OncoMechanome annotates cancer variants with the chain of molecular mechanisms that links the amino-acid change to the change in protein activity. Each chain is built step by step by a panel of LLM annotators, every step backed by a cited source, then consolidated by an arbiter.

This demo shows a sample of annotated variants. The full version, covering the complete set of gain-of-function variants, will be available soon.
