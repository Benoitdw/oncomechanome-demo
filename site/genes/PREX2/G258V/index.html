Wild type

PREX2 is a guanine nucleotide exchange factor that promotes the activation of the small GTPase RAC1 and independently binds to and inhibits the tumor suppressor PTEN. Its steady-state cellular abundance is regulated post-translationally through ubiquitin-dependent proteolysis, where binding to GNMT recruits the E3 ligase HectH9 to drive its degradation.

PREX2 G258V

The G258V substitution replaces a small, conformationally flexible glycine with a bulkier, beta-branched valine within the pleckstrin homology (PH) domain. While the specific structural effect on the domain's interactions is uncharacterized, the mutation slows the turnover of the protein.

This is a demo of OncoMechanome

OncoMechanome annotates cancer variants with the chain of molecular mechanisms that links the amino-acid change to the change in protein activity. Each chain is built step by step by a panel of LLM annotators, every step backed by a cited source, then consolidated by an arbiter.
