Wild type

SF3B1 is a core scaffold component of the U2 small nuclear ribonucleoprotein (snRNP) complex, which functions in early spliceosome assembly. Its HEAT-repeat domains bind the intronic branch point sequence in pre-mRNA and coordinate with other splicing factors to ensure accurate selection of the canonical 3' splice site.

SF3B1 K741Q

The substitution replaces a positively charged lysine with an uncharged glutamine at residue 741, located on the solvent-exposed surface of the HEAT repeat domain. This removes a positive charge but provides a side chain of near wild-type length, perturbing the local interaction surface without destroying the overall structural scaffold.

This is a demo of OncoMechanome

OncoMechanome annotates cancer variants with the chain of molecular mechanisms that links the amino-acid change to the change in protein activity. Each chain is built step by step by a panel of LLM annotators, every step backed by a cited source, then consolidated by an arbiter.

This demo shows a sample of annotated variants. The full version, covering the complete set of gain-of-function variants, will be available soon.
