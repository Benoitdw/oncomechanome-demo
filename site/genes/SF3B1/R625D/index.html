Wild type

SF3B1 is a core component of the U2 snRNP spliceosome complex that recognises the intron branchpoint sequence during pre-mRNA splicing. Its C-terminal HEAT-repeat domain contacts the pre-mRNA and serves as a binding platform for splicing factors, including the ATPase PRP5 (DDX46) which proofreads branch site selection, and the G-patch protein SUGP1 which activates the DHX15 helicase to displace SF1 from the canonical branchpoint.

SF3B1 R625D

The R625D substitution replaces a long, positively charged arginine with a short, negatively charged aspartate in the solvent-exposed RNA path of the HEAT-repeat domain (H4-H7 cluster). This charge reversal alters the local electrostatic surface, disrupting the hydrogen-bonding networks and complementarity required to dock the acidic loop of PRP5 and to bind SUGP1.

This is a demo of OncoMechanome

OncoMechanome annotates cancer variants with the chain of molecular mechanisms that links the amino-acid change to the change in protein activity. Each chain is built step by step by a panel of LLM annotators, every step backed by a cited source, then consolidated by an arbiter.
