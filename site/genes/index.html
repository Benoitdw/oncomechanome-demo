Label glyphs

One schematic per label, drawn once by hand and composed by the annotation. A per-variant drawing cannot be generated in a way worth trusting, but the vocabulary is closed — so the picture belongs to the label, and target supplies the caption, which is why one glyph serves every variant acting on a different partner.

They are deliberately abstract. A glyph illustrates a category; it is not a depiction of one variant's structure — that is the job of the structure track beside it.

This is a demo of OncoMechanome

OncoMechanome annotates cancer variants with the chain of molecular mechanisms that links the amino-acid change to the change in protein activity. Each chain is built step by step by a panel of LLM annotators, every step backed by a cited source, then consolidated by an arbiter.

This demo shows a sample of annotated variants. The full version, covering the complete set of gain-of-function variants, will be available soon.
